About Voss Group

Owner-operated. Washington State-based. Built for federal supply reliability.

Voss Group LLC is a Washington State-based wholesale industrial supply distribution and federal contracting company, formed February 24, 2026. Voss Group is building its business to serve the procurement needs of federal, state, and local government buyers through responsive industrial-supply sourcing and distribution.

Voss Group operates as a wholesale distributor and is structured to compete as a nonmanufacturer where applicable, subject to solicitation-specific requirements, SBA size standards, and 13 CFR 121.406. The Defense Logistics Agency is the company's primary federal target market. Based in Snohomish, Washington, Voss Group has regional proximity to PSNS Bremerton and DLA Maritime Puget Sound.

As an owner-operated company, Voss Group provides a direct point of contact for buyers. The company is building toward broader government contracting participation over time.
