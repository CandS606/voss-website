About Voss Group

Owner-operated. Washington State-based. Built for federal supply reliability.

Voss Group LLC is a Washington State-based wholesale industrial supply distribution and federal contracting company, formed February 24, 2026. The company was founded to serve the sustained procurement needs of federal, state, and local government buyers — supplying industrial hardware, safety equipment, and maintenance materials with a focus on reliability, compliance, and long-term contract performance.

Voss Group operates as a distributor under the SBA Nonmanufacturer Rule (13 CFR 121.406), sourcing from established manufacturers across seven confirmed product categories and delivering to government buyers with the Defense Logistics Agency as the dominant purchaser. The company's Snohomish, WA base positions it close to DLA Maritime Puget Sound and Puget Sound Naval Shipyard, the strongest regional match for its product lines.

As an owner-operated company, every order runs through a single accountable point of contact — no account-rep hand-offs, no call centers. Voss Group is built for the long game: establishing past performance as a subcontractor, growing toward prime contractor status, and pursuing SBA 8(a) Business Development Program eligibility as the company matures.
